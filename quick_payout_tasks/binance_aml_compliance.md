# Binance AML & Large-Volume Payout Compliance Protocol

## The Risk on Centralized Exchanges (Binance)
When dealing with large sums (such as $50,000 to $100,000+), centralized exchanges like Binance deploy strict automated **Anti-Money Laundering (AML)** and **Know Your Customer (KYC)** triggers. If a retail account suddenly receives large crypto transfers without prior high-volume history, Binance’s risk system may temporarily freeze withdrawals and request a **Source of Funds (SoF)** audit.

## How to Protect and Clear Large Payouts ($100k+)
1. **Official Bounty Receipts & Invoices:**
   - Platforms like Sherlock, Code4rena, and Immunefi provide official **Bounty Payout Receipts / Tax Invoices** upon issuing rewards. This is your bulletproof legal proof of where the money came from.
2. **Account Tier & VIP Status:**
   - Upgrade your Binance account to verified institutional or high-tier VIP status, declaring your profession as a "Smart Contract Security Auditor / Software Engineer" (backed by your Auto-Entrepreneur card).
3. **Over-The-Counter (OTC) Desks:**
   - For six-figure payouts ($100k+), do not dump funds through standard retail P2P. Use compliant **OTC Desks** or institutional liquidation channels where you provide your Immunefi/Sherlock audit certificate beforehand to ensure smooth, unhindered liquidity.
