# Algeria Payout Liquidation & Compliance Strategy

## The Core Reality of Web3 & Bounty Payouts
1. **No Direct Bank Wires:** Platforms like Sherlock, Code4rena, Immunefi, and Algora **do not** send traditional wire transfers from foreign banks to local bank accounts in Algeria. 
2. **Stablecoin / Crypto Escrow:** Payouts are made in **USDC** (a digital dollar stablecoin backed 1:1 by USD) or native crypto (ETH, XTM) directly to your non-custodial Web3 wallet (**MetaMask**).

## How to Handle Liquidity & Local Withdrawal Safely
1. **Self-Custody Safety:** Your funds remain 100% safe, private, and under your absolute control in your MetaMask wallet on the blockchain. No local bank or authority has visibility over your crypto wallet balances.
2. **Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Liquidity:** When you need cash in Algeria (DZD), crypto-native freelancers use trusted P2P platforms (like Binance P2P) or local secure OTC networks to convert USDC into local currency or digital payment methods.
3. **Legal Formalization (The "Auto-Entrepreneur" Card / المقاول الذاتي):**
   - Algeria officially introduced the **Card Auto-Entrepreneur (بطاقة المقاول الذاتي)** to legalize digital freelance income, export of digital services, and software development.
   - Registering under this legal framework allows you to officially declare software/security consulting income, obtain a tax ID, and legally receive and justify digital earnings without legal friction.
